#### Project Support

We can also help you plan your study: defining the sampling plan, setting pre-analytical procedures for sample collection and storage, and running additional statistical analyses or in-depth interpretation. These activities are agreed and quoted separately for each project.

#### How it works

You send plasma, serum, other biofluids or extracted RNA to our laboratory in Siena, following the preparation and shipping instructions we agree with you. When samples arrive, we register them and check that they meet the requirements for the requested analysis. Every quote reflects the number of samples and the steps you need.
