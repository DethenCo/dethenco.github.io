The Spin-off also received the special mention for female entrepreneurship and the sponsor award from Addì, and will represent the University of Siena at the national final in Bari.

DeThenCo, a spin-off developed by our research group at the Department of Medicine, Surgery and Neuroscience of the University of Siena, finished third at Start Cup Toscana 2026. The competition, organised by the Tuscan universities together with Regione Toscana, rewards innovative business ideas that come out of academic research. This year's awards were presented on 19 October at the Scuola IMT Alti Studi Lucca.


## Three awards in one evening

- **Third place** :: Overall ranking in Start Cup Toscana 2026, with a prize of €2,000.
- **Special mention** :: Recognition for female entrepreneurship.
- **Sponsor award** :: A strategic innovation assessment with the team at Addì Srl, Livorno.

![Addì sponsor award: a strategic innovation assessment for DeThenCo](/images/news/startcup2026_addi-strategic-assessment_dethenco.jpg)

## Next stop: Bari

With this result, the team will represent the University of Siena at the final of the Premio Nazionale per l'Innovazione (PNI), held in Bari on 3 and 4 December 2026.


Congratulations to everyone involved, and thanks to the organisers, the partner universities, and the sponsors for their support.
