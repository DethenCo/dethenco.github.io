DeThenCo — Decoding the Non-Coding — received the Giuliani Award at the XVII Forum of Borsa della Ricerca 2026, held in Caserta from 18 to 22 October. The recognition celebrates a project built around a simple idea: biologically different patients should not be treated as if they were the same.

Through DeThenCo, the team aims to decode circulating non-coding RNAs to identify molecular biomarkers for patient stratification, supporting precision medicine in immune-mediated diseases and immunotherapies.

The award belongs to the whole founding team behind the project — Francesco Dotta, Guido Sebastiani, and Daniela Fignani — together with the operating team, Elena Aiello, Mohamad Elrifai, and Erika Pedace, who are turning the idea into something concrete.

A very special moment for DeThenCo — Decoding the Non-Coding.
